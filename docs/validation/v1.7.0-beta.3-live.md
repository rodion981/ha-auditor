## Failed acceptance: introductory risk sentence

The HACS finding excerpt was:

> As a major version bump, this release includes breaking changes:

The [2.0.0 release](https://github.com/hacs/integration/releases/tag/2.0.0)
introduces its actual change list with this sentence. Although better source
provenance works, the excerpt still does not identify the concrete change.
The native to-do item and both template outputs reproduce this limitation.
Do not count concrete risk extraction as fully validated or promote this beta
to stable on the basis of its green automated suite.

Cause: `summarize_release` recognizes bare section labels but treats this
keyword-bearing introductory sentence as complete evidence, returning it
before reading the following list. The focused follow-up is to recognize
risk-list lead-ins and extract their items without crossing the next section.
Add this exact structural case to the regression suite before another beta.
